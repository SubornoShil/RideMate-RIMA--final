How a ride gets tied together

Four steps. Nobody sees your phone number until you say yes.

{% include "partials/lane.html" with id="lane-how" kind="car" mode="scroll" %}
  1. 01

    Post the trip you are already making

    Pick-up, destination, time, vehicle, seats and the fare per person. Zero means the fare is shared on the day. Women can mark a ride women-only.

    Example

    FromDhaka Gabtoli
    ToChattogram GEC Circle
    WhenFri 6:00 AM
    Seats · Fare3 · Tk 650

    Post ride

  2. 02

    Riders ask for a seat, you decide

    Each request shows the rider's trust score. Accept or decline from your dashboard. The last seat can never be given to two people.

    Example

    S
    Suborno Shil100.0 Excellent
    Accept
    F
    Famin Khan92.0 Excellent
    Accept
    R
    Rafa AmonNew member
    Accept

    Seats left: 3

  3. 03

    Numbers unlock, guardians are told

    After you accept, you and the rider see each other's phone numbers. Nobody else does. If the rider has Guardian Mode on, their people get the route and time by SMS.

    Example

    F
    Famin Khan01711 ••• •••

    SMS to guardian: Famin rides Dhaka Gabtoli to Chattogram, Fri 6:00 AM.

    Seats left: 2

  4. 04

    Ride, split the fare, rate your ride partner

    When the host marks the ride complete, everyone on it can rate everyone else once. Ratings from brand-new accounts count half, so a fresh account cannot rewrite anyone's reputation.

    Example

    R
    Rate Rafa AmonYour ride partner

    ★★★★★

    100.0 Excellent

    Trust score = weighted average of ratings

Trust is the product

Verified Circles
Join your neighbourhood, office or campus with a code. Rides from people in your circle come first.
Women-only rides
Created, shown and requested by women accounts only. The server enforces it, not the page.
Guardian Mode
Add up to three people. When a seat is confirmed, they get the route and departure time by SMS.
A score you can read
Ratings count for more once an account is a week old, so a fresh account cannot rewrite anyone's reputation.
